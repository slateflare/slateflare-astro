When starting a business online, one of the first decisions is choosing between a landing page and a full website.

They serve different purposes.

Understanding the difference can help you choose the right approach for your goals.

## What Is a Landing Page?

A landing page is typically designed around a specific goal.

That goal might be:

- Generating leads
- Promoting a service
- Launching a product
- Collecting signups
- Promoting an event
- Getting people to contact you

Because the page has a focused purpose, the content and design can be structured around a specific action.

## What Is a Website?

A website usually contains multiple pages and provides broader information about a business.

For example, a business website might include:

- Home
- About
- Services
- Portfolio
- Blog
- Contact
- FAQ

A website is useful when visitors need to explore different parts of your business.

## When a Landing Page Makes Sense

A landing page can be a good choice when you're:

### Launching a New Service

If you're introducing a specific service, a focused page can explain the offer without requiring visitors to navigate through an entire website.

### Running a Marketing Campaign

Advertising campaigns often benefit from dedicated landing pages because the page can closely match the campaign's message.

### Testing an Idea

If you're validating a new product or service, a landing page can provide an online presence without requiring a large website.

### Starting a New Business

You may not need ten pages on day one.

A focused landing page can communicate your offer while you build the rest of your business.

## When a Full Website Makes Sense

A larger website may be more appropriate when your business has:

- Multiple services
- A large portfolio
- Extensive resources
- Several audiences
- A growing content strategy
- Complex navigation requirements

A website gives visitors more opportunities to explore your business.

## You Can Have Both

A landing page and website aren't mutually exclusive.

A business can have a full website while also creating dedicated landing pages for individual campaigns, services, or products.

For example:

```text
example.com
    |
    ├── About
    ├── Services
    ├── Portfolio
    ├── Blog
    └── Contact

Campaign
    |
    └── example.com/special-offer
```
